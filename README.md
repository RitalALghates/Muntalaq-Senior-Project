Muntalaq – AI-Powered Academic Mentorship & Learning Platform
Connecting university students with graduates and AI to create a more personalized academic journey.
Muntalaq is an AI-powered academic mentorship and learning platform developed as a Senior Project in Software Engineering at the University of Jeddah.
The platform aims to support university students through academic guidance, mentorship, learning resources, and AI-assisted support.
________________________________________
Problem
University students may face difficulties when looking for:
•	Accessible academic guidance
•	Suitable mentors and graduates to connect with
•	Relevant learning resources
•	Personalized academic support
•	A centralized environment for academic communication and support
Muntalaq was designed to bring these services together in one platform.
________________________________________
Solution
Muntalaq provides an integrated environment that brings together academic support, mentorship, learning resources, and AI-assisted services.
________________________________________
Key Features
•	AI-assisted academic support and personalized guidance.
•	A mentorship environment that facilitates connections between university students and graduates.
•	Curated learning resources and educational content.
•	Community and communication features that support academic interaction.
•	User dashboards for managing and tracking academic activities.
•	Intelligent features designed to enhance the overall academic experience.
